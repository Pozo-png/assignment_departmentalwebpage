About Department

The B.A. (Honours) Economics programme of SVC is much sought after by students from across the country. The Department also offers B.A. (Programme) with Economics as Major, and a Minor in Economics for students of other disciplines.

All programmes follow the Undergraduate Curricular Framework 2022 (UGCF) under NEP 2020 and the curriculum set by the Department of Economics, Delhi School of Economics.

Activities

Lectures, workshops and symposia

The Department runs knowledge and skill enrichment events, including:

  • Distinguished Lectures by eminent persons from the academic, government and corporate sectors
  • Workshops
  • Alumni Inspire Sessions
  • Symposia

Past speakers include Dr. Manmohan Singh, Shri Montek Singh Ahluwalia, Dr. C. Rangarajan, Prof. K.L. Krishna, Dr. Shankar Acharya, Dr. M. Govinda Rao, Dr. David C. Mulford, Prof. K. Srinath Reddy, Prof. Pami Dua and Prof. Shreekant Gupta. More recent guests include Dr. Arvind Virmani, Dr. K.K. Tripathy, Prof. Ram Singh, Prof. Rohini Somanathan and Prof. Aditya Bhattacharjea.

Economics Association

The student body organises the annual fest BLISSPOINT, which draws national and international participation. At the fest, the annual magazine ECON EXPRESSIONS is released, with student articles on contemporary social, political and economic issues.
